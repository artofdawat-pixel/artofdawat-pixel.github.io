The meaning
Dawat
Urdu·an invitation·a feast

A dawat is an invitation to sit, eat and stay a while. Art of Dawat brings that spirit to evenings out in New York City: a warm welcome, good company, and the feeling that you were expected.

Your host

Sabrina Khan

Sabrina started Art of Dawat to bring people together the way a good host does: with care, good food, and a seat for everyone.

Questions

Before you arrive

Who are these evenings for?

Art of Dawat is rooted in Muslim hospitality, and everyone is welcome at the table. Come curious, come kind, and come hungry.

Is alcohol served?

No. Every Art of Dawat event is alcohol-free.

Can I come on my own?

Yes. Many guests do. Every evening is hosted, and we make introductions so no one is left standing alone.

What does the iftar ticket include?

An iftar dinner served at sunset, followed by chai, coffee and dessert.

How do tickets work?

You buy your seat here with secure card checkout. Your emailed receipt is your ticket, and you check in by name at the door.

Where are events held?

At restaurants and venues in New York City. The venue for each event is announced on this page and in your ticket email.

Can I partner with or sponsor an event?

We'd love that. Email info@artofdawat.com with a little about you or your space.
