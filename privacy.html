Privacy

Art of Dawat LLC ("Art of Dawat", "we") runs artofdawat.com and hosts social events in New York City. This page explains what information we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • What you send us. If you email us, we receive your name, email address and whatever you choose to write.
  • Ticket purchases. When tickets go on sale, card payments are processed by Stripe. We receive your name, email address and the details of your order. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Guest list. If you ask to join our guest list, we keep your email address to tell you about upcoming events.

How we use it

  • To confirm your ticket and check you in at the door.
  • To send you event details and, if you asked, news about future events.
  • To answer your questions.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone except the services that help us run events, such as our payment processor and email provider, and only for that purpose.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time, or ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing info@artofdawat.com.

Website basics

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses fonts served by Google Fonts. Like most websites, those services may receive standard technical information such as your IP address when the page loads. We don't use advertising trackers.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
